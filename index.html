Stories

  • Family story from Norma: Norma's parents, Thomas and Alice, moved their own house to make room for the house Norma and Dominic built on the other side of the property (Orient Way, Rutherford; details to confirm). Norma's mortgage was $141 (per month, to confirm), and she had palpitations worrying she couldn't afford it. Around then Doc's income was about $60,000 a year (clarified by Tim and Norma; figure to confirm). The lot was 100 by 182 feet; Norma needed 50 feet to subdivide and build, so her parents moved their house about 5 feet to free up the footage. Interview
  • For her 99th birthday in July 2013, Alice rode a party bus and "pole danced" her way to a Yankee game to see Derek Jeter. Her obituary says she was famous for her gnocchi and was "a real joker". Obituary
  • In 1957 Elizabeth (Baratta) Mazzaccaro died of a heart attack in Philadelphia while visiting her daughter Mary Ann after an operation. She had lived about 50 years at 8 Connell Street, Old Forge, near the Sparrowville coal mine.
  • Pat Mazzaccaro Jr. played the cordovox in a band called the Trail Riders. Obituary
  • Doc's son Dominick has served about 18 years on the Wood-Ridge Borough Council (likely the same person, based on matching family names).
